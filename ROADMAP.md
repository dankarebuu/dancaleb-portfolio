## Development Rules

1. Complete the current phase before moving to the next, unless a dependency requires otherwise.
2. Do not mark tasks completed until they have been tested or verified.
3. Update this roadmap whenever a milestone is completed.
4. Keep PROJECT_CONTEXT.md updated when major project decisions change.
5. Keep DESIGN_SYSTEM.md consistent with actual implementation.
6. Avoid unnecessary scope expansion during the initial launch.
7. Prioritize a functional, polished portfolio over excessive features.
